## Model assumptions
The 100 MW / 10 GWh grid-storage model assumed round-trip efficiency of 70 percent for a 100 h discharge duration; this AC-to-AC value was not measured and was held constant over the project life.

## Sensitivity analysis
The synthetic LCOS calculation is sensitive to efficiency, lifetime, charging price, and capital cost.
