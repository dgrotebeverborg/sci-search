A few good questions

Before you dive in.

Is this searching the literature or chatting with an AI?

Sci-Search discovers publications, acquires available open full texts, and extracts evidence. Optional Mistral analysis adds synthesis after local citation and quotation validation. Without it, the app uses an extractive evidence baseline.

Can I inspect what an answer is based on?

Yes. Findings link to exact passages and source documents. Search strategies, candidate screening, and inclusion decisions remain available, and researchers can record their own review decisions.

Does it read papers behind a paywall?

Live acquisition uses available open full texts. Unavailable candidates stay visible. Researchers can supply a local copy when authorized to do so.

Is the result a systematic review?

It is an AI-assisted evidence review. Source-grounding checks do not establish scientific correctness, and the prototype does not replace expert assessment or the formal protocol and screening requirements of a systematic review.

Evidence, up close

Follow a finding back to its source.

In Sci-Search, an evidence record brings together:

  • The exact passage supporting a finding
  • Publication and source-document details
  • Section, page when available, and text offsets
  • Extraction provenance and researcher review status
A finding is the beginning of the trail. The full text is there when you need the context.
